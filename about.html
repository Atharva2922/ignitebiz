About IgniteBiz AI

Empowering the future through the world’s largest AI-powered venture studio platform for talent transformation, entrepreneurship, and skill development.

Our Genesis & Philosophy

Rooted in Indian Wisdom, Driven by Society 5.0

"IGNITING BUSINESS YOGA" is the world’s largest AI powered strategic initiative aimed at transforming human potential through entrepreneurship and future-skill training. Rooted in the perennial principles of Indian wisdom (‘सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः।’) and inspired by the Society 5.0 paradigm, this program integrates a collaborative model of digital innovation, psychometric science, and startup venture studio development.

Operated as a dedicated digital platform under the CSR initiative of Excellent Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd., we are closely affiliated with Swavlambi Bharat Abhiyan, Vivekananda Youth Connect, Indo Arab Ventures, and the Indian National Students’ Association (INSA) UK to prepare youth and institutions for Vision 2030.

Rated 5-Star by Our Learners & Partners Recognized for transformative psychometric accuracy and structured venture incubation.
👁

Our Vision

To establish the world’s largest Entrepreneurship and Skillonomics Venture Studio that empowers individuals with entrepreneurial capability and future-ready skills to build a sustainable, inclusive, and technologically driven global society of Vision 2030.

🎯

Our Mission

  • ✓ Conduct 10 crore psychometric assessments across global youth and professionals in the next 2 years.
  • ✓ Train 50 lakh entrepreneurs with end-to-end financial and seed incubation support.
  • ✓ Ensure 5 crore skilled professionals are placed as per real-time industry demands.
  • ✓ Establish AI & IoT Labs with industry-academia collaboration across 50+ partner institutions.
