Step 1 • Eligibility Screening

Comprehensive IPO Readiness Diagnostic

Going public is an eligibility-dependent institutional milestone. We benchmark your enterprise against regulatory listing norms across BSE SME, NSE Emerge, and Mainboard exchanges.

BSE SME / NSE Emerge For Scaleups

SME Platform Listing Framework

Designed for high-growth enterprises seeking initial public market validation and growth capital with streamlined regulatory overhead.

Paid-up Capital: Post-issue capital up to ₹25 Crore
Operating Track Record: Minimum 2 to 3 years operational history
Profitability (PAT): Positive operating profit in at least 2 of 3 years
Underwriting: 100% underwritten with merchant banker market making (3 yrs)
Reporting: Half-yearly financial reporting format
BSE / NSE Mainboard Enterprise Scale

Mainboard Institutional Listing

The definitive listing platform for large-scale enterprises with institutional track records, deep liquidity, and global mutual fund participation.

Paid-up Capital: Minimum post-issue capital > ₹10 Crore
Market Capitalization: Minimum issue size ₹25 Crore+
Operating Track Record: Minimum 3 consecutive years profitable track record
Tangible Assets: Minimum ₹3 Crore in net tangible assets in each preceding year
Reporting: Quarterly Ind-AS compliance with limited review reports
Institutional Integrity

Board Governance & Internal Financial Controls (IFC)

Public markets penalize governance deficits. We structure your corporate governance architecture to meet SEBI LODR (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) benchmarks.

👔 Independent Board of Directors: Onboarding qualified independent directors, woman directors, and domain stalwarts to balance founder control.
📊 Board Committees: Formal constitution of Audit Committee, Nomination & Remuneration Committee (NRC), and Stakeholders Relationship Committee (SRC).
🛡️ Internal Financial Controls (IFC): Documented risk control matrices, enterprise ERP controls, and automated fraud-prevention workflows.
📜 Secretarial Audit: Pre-IPO compliance audit under Section 204 of Companies Act 2013 by certified practicing Company Secretaries.
🌱 ESG Disclosures: Foundational Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reporting (BRSR) framework to attract ESG-focused funds.
Audit Rigor

Financial Restatement & Legal Diligence Trail

Eliminate diligence red flags before merchant bankers open books to institutional anchor investors.

📊

Restated Ind-AS Financials

Conversion and restatement of the preceding 3 financial years into standardized Ind-AS formats with audited reconciliation statements.

📜

Clean Title Diligence

Comprehensive verification of real estate titles, leasehold agreements, IPR patent ownership, vendor contracts, and labor compliance certificates.

⚖️

Litigation Resolution

Quantification, provisioning, or pre-DRHP settlement of pending commercial disputes, tax demands, and contingent liabilities.

IPO Advisory Syndicate Components

Category-I Merchant Bankers: Issue structuring, DRHP drafting, price band design, and lead coordination with stock exchanges.
IPO Legal Counsel: Legal due diligence, promoter background verification, material litigation disclosure, and DRHP legal certification.
Peer-Reviewed Statutory Auditors: Independent examination and issuance of comfort letters on restated financial statements.
Registrar & Share Transfer Agent (RTA): Allotment basis formulation, demat credit processing, and investor grievance handling.

Execution Timeline

The 180-Day SME Listing Roadmap

A disciplined, milestone-gated timeline from initial readiness audit to ringing the opening bell on the stock exchange.

01

Month 1: Diagnostic & Corporate Restructuring

Capital structure re-alignment, bonus share issuance, conversion from Pvt Ltd to Public Limited, and appointment of independent directors.

02

Month 2: Syndicate Appointment & Due Diligence

Formal onboarding of Category-I Merchant Banker, Legal Counsel, and Peer-Reviewed Statutory Auditor. Initiation of legal and financial due diligence.

03

Month 3: DRHP Drafting & Audit Sign-off

Preparation of the Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP). Statutory auditor restates financials for past 3 years. Risk factors and business model codified.

04

Month 4: Exchange Filing & In-Principle Approval

DRHP submitted to BSE SME / NSE Emerge exchange committees. Site inspection by exchange officials and reply to observation queries.

05

Month 5: Roadshows, Price Band & RHP Filing

Promoter roadshows for institutional buyers and anchor investors. Price band finalized with merchant banker and Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) filed with RoC.

06

Month 6: Issue Opens, Allotment & Listing Bell

Public issue open for 3 business days. Subscription tracking, basis of allotment approval by stock exchange, electronic share credit, and listing day ceremony.
