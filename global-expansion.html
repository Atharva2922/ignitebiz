Step 1 • International Audit

Export & Global Expansion Readiness Diagnostic

Expanding overseas without evidence multiplies burn. Our 4-pillar readiness diagnostic benchmarks whether your product, unit economics, and governance are ready for cross-border flight.

Proven customer retention, stable monthly recurring revenue (MRR), and gross margins exceeding 60% in the home market before deploying capital overseas.

Capacity to scale cloud infrastructure, localized multi-lingual support, or physical export fulfillment without degrading domestic service level agreements.

📜

3. Regulatory Certifications

Pre-requisite international standards compliance including CE marking, UKCA, US FDA registrations, ISO 27001, SOC-2 Type II, and GDPR data privacy.

⚖️

4. Transfer Pricing & FEMA

Arms-length transfer pricing policies, intellectual property licensing architecture, and RBI Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) regulatory clearances.

Step 2 • Go-to-Market Model

Select the Optimal Cross-Border Entry Architecture

Every market demands a calibrated balance between capital expenditure, speed to market, and operational control. We architect the right vehicle for your venture stage.

🏢 Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS): Full operational control, 100% equity retention, direct local contracts, and corporate brand equity. Best for mature scaleups.
🤝 Joint Venture (JV): Partner with an established in-country player to leverage existing distribution licenses, local relationships, and government contracts.
📦 Authorized Master Distributor: Fast, low-capital market penetration through screened regional distributors operating on performance-based margins.
💻 Digital Cross-Border Merchant: Direct software/SaaS delivery from India using international merchant-of-record (MoR) infrastructure like Paddle or Stripe.

Cross-Border Regulatory Gateways
