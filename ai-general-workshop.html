Agentic AI General Workshop

Session objectives for managers, officers, and executives: Learn and apply autonomous multi-agent systems in 4–5 hours of hands-on practice.

Hands-on Executive Immersion

What Managers Will Learn & Apply

Moving beyond simple ChatGPT prompts to orchestrating goal-driven autonomous software agents.

01

Understand Agentic AI

Core concepts and how autonomous agentic systems fundamentally differ from static generative models.

02

Think Like an AI Manager

Translate business goals into agent roles, tool permissions, verification guardrails, and KPI budgets.

03

Design Autonomous Workflows

Map decision paths, define fallback criteria, set human-in-the-loop gates, and optimize latency.

04

Build Agents with Free Tools

Hands-on construction of multi-step research and data gathering agents with accessible no-code/low-code toolsets.

05

Apply AI to Operations

Evaluate outputs objectively, monitor agent accuracy, and embed agentic loops into existing CRM and ERP workflows.

06

Why Strategy Matters

Align executive directives with real-world department constraints to prevent prompt drift and hallucination risks.

07

Rapid Deployment Roadmap

Map pilot opportunities in your firm, set evaluation metrics, and run zero-risk sandbox experiments.

08

Overcome Challenges

Address integration gaps, executive buy-in hurdles, and agent observability with proven enterprise patterns.
