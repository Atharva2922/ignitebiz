## Frequently Asked Questions
We understand that starting a psychometric assessment can feel like a big step, and you may have some questions before getting started.
How long does the psychometric test take?
The assessment is designed to be thorough yet manageable, typically completed in one session, taking approximately 45–60 minutes depending on the test type.
What does the psychometric test evaluate?
The test evaluates 12 key dimensions, including emotional intelligence, problem-solving, learning agility, risk tolerance, entrepreneurial mindset, business acumen, operational skills, creative expression, interpersonal and organizational skills, among others.
Is my test confidential?
Yes! Your results are completely confidential and shared only with you. We maintain a secure and private platform to protect your data.
How will I receive my results?
After completing the test, you will receive a detailed personalized report with insights, strengths, and recommendations to guide your personal and professional growth.
Do I need multiple sessions?
The test is a one-time comprehensive assessment, but you can retake it or access additional training and resources anytime through our platform to track your progress and development.
