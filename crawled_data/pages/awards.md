# Global Business Innovation Awards competition
Recognizing the best business ideas and concepts from around the world, the Global Innovation Awards celebrate creativity, entrepreneurship, and transformative solutions.   
  
**After any one or both psychometric tests, you may become eligible to participate in the global business innovation awards competition.**  
Submit your innovative business idea or concept and receive expert feedback and refinement. This platform brings together some of the best business ideas and concepts from around the world. The Global Innovation Awards celebrate creativity, entrepreneurship, and transformative solutions that drive global impact. 
This competition is in**3 round** :  
  
**1. State level Round  
2. National Level Round  
3. International or Global round**
![](https://ignitebizai.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Global_Innovation-_Awards.webp)
## Awards & Prizes
**1st Prize** : $100,000
**2nd Prize** : $50,000
**3rd Prize** : $25,000
**Social Innovation Award** : $25,000
**Regional Business Innovation Awards** : $10,000 per region

Winners will be selected through four rigorous rounds in the global competition, ensuring merit- based recognition of the most innovative ideas. Business idea should be Pitch deck Sequa template format, with Close Circuit Testing (Cost efficiency, Quality assurance and Scalability) and Market Entry Roadmaps
