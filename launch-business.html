Step 1 • Corporate Architecture

Choose the Right Legal Structure for Your Venture

Your entity structure defines liability, tax burden, equity distribution, and eligibility for institutional seed capital.

🏢
Recommended for Startups

Private Limited (Pvt Ltd)

The gold standard for venture-backed companies. Separate legal identity, limited liability, and ability to issue equity shares, ESOPs, and convertible notes to angel/VC investors.

  • ✓ Required for VC/Angel Equity Funding
  • ✓ Eligible for DPIIT 80-IAC 3-Year Tax Holiday
  • ✓ Seamless ESOP pool creation for key talent
  • ✓ Perpetual succession & transferability
🤝

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Combines partnership flexibility with limited liability protection. Ideal for service agencies, consulting practices, and bootstrapping founders with low statutory overhead.

  • ✓ Lower compliance burden than Pvt Ltd
  • ✓ No mandatory statutory audit under ₹40L turnover
  • ✓ No dividend distribution tax
  • × Cannot issue equity shares or stock options
👤

One Person Company (OPC)

Enables a solitary founder to operate with corporate limited liability without requiring a second co-founder or co-director at inception.

  • ✓ 100% single-shareholder ownership
  • ✓ Separate corporate legal entity
  • ✓ Easy conversion to Pvt Ltd as team expands
  • × Requires nominee director designation
⚡

Sole Proprietorship

Fastest and least expensive way to test a local idea. Unincorporated status with zero formal MCA registration, but carries unlimited personal liability.

  • ✓ Zero incorporation filing fees
  • ✓ Simplest tax filing under individual PAN
  • × Personal assets at risk for business debts
  • × Not eligible for Startup India grants or VC funding
Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA)

End-to-End SPICe+ Company Incorporation Workflow

We eliminate bureaucratic hurdles by coordinating directly with practicing Company Secretaries (CS) and Chartered Accountants (CA) on the government MCA21 portal.

1. SPICe+ Part A: Name reservation with dual-choice availability check against trademark class registers.
2. Digital Signature (DSC): Class-3 encrypted DSC issuance for all founding directors.
3. SPICe+ Part B: Integrated filing for Director Identification Numbers (DIN), Certificate of Incorporation (CoI), PAN, and TAN.
4. Constitutional Charters: Tailored electronic drafting of Memorandum of Association (e-MoA) and Articles of Association (e-AoA).
5. AGILE-PRO-S: Mandatory integrated registrations for EPFO, ESIC, Professional Tax, and immediate Bank Account pre-clearance.
