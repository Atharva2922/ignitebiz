The Science: Iris & Brain Connection

The brain is the only organ directly connected with our eyes. The nerve that directly connects our eyes with our cerebral cortex is called the Optical Nerve.

  • ✓ Iris Analysis is the scientific study of patterns on the Iris, the colored part of your eyes.
  • ✓ The iris is one of the most intricate and fascinating tissue structures in the human body.
  • ✓ Iridology provides accurate insights into physical health, cognitive patterns, and inherent qualities.
  • ✓ Eye movement is required for every function of the brain; the brain cannot process thoughts without micro-movements of the eye.
