⚖️ Scientific Methodology, Ethical Boundaries & Data Privacy

The IgniteBiz AI 12-Dimension Psychometric Evaluation is designed as a developmental guidance tool. It evaluates cognitive, emotional, and execution traits across validated psychometric scales. The assessment does not constitute a clinical psychiatric diagnosis, nor does it guarantee entrepreneurial commercial success. Every participant possesses agency to pursue either the Entrepreneurship Track or Skill Development Track regardless of baseline scores; human counselors and mentors are available to review scores upon request.

All candidate diagnostic records are protected under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. Data is stored on encrypted Indian servers, is never sold to commercial third parties, and is accessible only to the candidate and authorized studio mentors with explicit consent.
